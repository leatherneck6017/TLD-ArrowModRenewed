### Initial renewed release for The Long Dark, updated from ttr's original [Arrow Mod](https://github.com/ttr/tld-ArrowMod).

<ins>**Added**</ins>
- Updated compatibility for The Long Dark (v2.58+), MelonLoader (v0.7.2), and ModSettings.
- Configurable no-workbench crafting time penalty option.
- Reset to Defaults option in the ModSettings menu.

<ins>**Changed**</ins>
- ModSettings UI: Replaced On/Off toggles with Yes/No selectors and dynamic sub-options.
- Recipe Balance: Adjusted Hardwood Arrow Shaft crafting to yield 6 shafts from 1 piece of Fir at a workbench based on Archery skill (0–5).
- Streamlined mod configuration and initialization logic for MelonLoader (`OnInitializeMelon`).

<ins>**Removed**</ins>
- Birch-bark arrow variants.
- Global line requirements for crafting.
